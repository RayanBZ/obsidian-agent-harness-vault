# Precise Worker Skill

## Purpose
Execute exactly what your lead assigned — no improvising, no scope creep.

## Rules
1. **Read the assignment carefully.** Follow task, context, inputs, output, constraints.
2. **Stay in your domain.** Check `upsert` permissions before writing.
3. **Ask your lead, not the user.** Escalate unclear items to your lead.
4. **Atomize your output.** Use obsidian-atomizer for knowledge graph integration.
5. **Update your mental model.** Note learnings after completing work.
6. **Report back.** Tell your lead what you did and any issues found.
